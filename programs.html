Programs

Education and convening, open to all.

Four program areas that widen access to the knowledge, networks, and resources climate innovation depends on.

Program

Public and donor education

Learning sessions and educational content on early-stage climate innovation, cleantech, and impact pathways. Open to the public and to donors who want to understand how philanthropic capital moves climate solutions forward.

Includes

  • Public learning sessions on climate innovation and cleantech
  • Educational content on technology, market, and impact pathways
  • Sessions for donors on philanthropic capital in climate innovation

Program

Founder and entrepreneur support

Education and engagement for early-stage founders: innovation readiness, investment preparedness, office hours, and help navigating the ecosystem of networks and resources.

Includes

  • Founder education on raising early-stage capital
  • Office hours with experienced operators and investors
  • Guidance on grants, programs, and partners across the ecosystem

Program

Public events and convenings

Events that bring founders, investors, researchers, partners, and the public together to share knowledge and build relationships, including programming virtually and in person across the US and beyond.

Includes

  • Climate Week sessions in the Pacific Northwest, New York, San Francisco, and beyond
  • Public events across the regional and national cleantech ecosystem
  • Convenings that connect founders, investors, researchers, and partners

Program

Sector catalyst initiatives

Partnerships and pilots that strengthen the broader climate innovation sector, including cross-sector collaboration, workforce-adjacent programs, and ecosystem research.

Includes

  • Cross-sector partnerships with universities, accelerators, and industry
  • Workforce-adjacent programs
  • Ecosystem research and pilots
